About

I'm a co-founder and the CTO of Senecta, where we help home care agencies look after their clients between visits. I design our hardware, from the boards to the enclosures, with AI agents doing much of the work: placing and checking boards, reviewing them before they go to the fab, and handing each design over as a record that a person or an agent can check.

The tools that made that work are open source below.

Open source

hwkit Python · MIT

Hand a hardware design to a person or an AI agent as one checked record: a schema, numeric geometry gates and one 3D viewer, for 3D-printed parts and KiCad boards.

jlc-agent Python · MCP · MIT

JLCPCB from the command line and from AI agents, plus the checks before you order: every part's rotation against JLC's own footprint, and a preflight of the order against the board.

sessionlift Python · MIT

Move a live Claude Code session from your laptop to your own server and back, and keep driving it from the Claude app while the laptop is shut.
